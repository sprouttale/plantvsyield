Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

What we collect

When you join the whitelist we store your X username, X user ID, display name, profile picture URL, the links of your quote and comment posts, and the public wallet address you connect. When you play, we store your wallet address and in-game progress (such as coins, waves and actions) to run the game, prevent cheating and distribute rewards.

What we do not collect

We never ask for or store your password, seed phrase or private keys. We do not post on X on your behalf and we do not read your direct messages.

How we use it

Only to manage the whitelist, operate the game, detect abuse and contact winners publicly on X. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except the infrastructure providers that host it (Supabase, GitHub Pages).

Deleting your data

Message @PlantVsYield on X to have your whitelist entry and game data removed.
